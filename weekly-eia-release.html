Release schedule, in season

  • October first Monday through March last Monday: weekly Monday release, ~13:00 ET.
  • Holiday shift: if Monday is a federal holiday (e.g. MLK Day, Presidents Day) the release moves to Tuesday.
  • National + state averages + PADD region averages publish together.

Off-season pause

Between the last March release and the first October release, the SHOPP series is dark. That is ~26 weeks per year with no fresh retail data. Other major news sites continue quoting the March number through summer, which is how you get "heating oil prices" headlines in August citing data 5 months old. EIA

How this site stays fresh in the pause

Daily NYMEX NY Harbor ULSD settlement runs year-round. The wholesale-vs-retail page shows the live spread. Dealer programme pages cite the most recent in-season retail and pin the off-season stamp at the top, no pretending it is current.

FAQ

What day does EIA publish the heating oil price?
Mondays around 13:00 Eastern, October through March. If Monday is a federal holiday, the release shifts to Tuesday.
Why does EIA pause heating oil prices in summer?
The SHOPP (State Heating Oil and Propane Program) survey runs only during the residential heating season. Surveying dealers in July yields stale, non-residential pricing. The series resumes in October.
How fresh is the price on this site between releases?
Between weekly EIA releases, the NYMEX NY Harbor ULSD front-month settles daily. This site shows both: the EIA retail (weekly) and the NYMEX wholesale (daily) so the dashboard never goes stale during the season.
