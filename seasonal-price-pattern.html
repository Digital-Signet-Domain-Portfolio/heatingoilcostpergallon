The August pre-buy window

Aug-Sep prices average 5 to 8% below the season peak. Dealers offer pre-buy contracts at summer rack prices. Risk: if crude crashes between August lock and December burn, you overpay. Reward: a normal-to-cold winter rewards the lock. Run the break-even.

What weather pattern reliably moves prices

Two events: NOAA winter outlook release in mid-October (forecast La Niña or El Niño bias) and the first sustained Arctic outbreak in PADD1A. Both can move retail $0.10 to $0.30/gal in 3 weeks.NOAA
