Connecticut all-time high

Connecticut's post-2020 peak was the 2022-23 season at $5.74/gal, driven by the Russia-Ukraine distillate shortage. The 25-year nominal high was 2008, around $4.50/gal in Connecticut residential.

vs PADD1A

Connecticut tracks PADD1A within ±$0.15/gal across seasons. 31% of CT homes heat with oil, the highest oil-heat penetration in the lower NE. CT DEEP publishes a separate weekly survey.
