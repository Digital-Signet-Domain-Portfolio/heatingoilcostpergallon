Connecticut heating oil dealer programmes

Most Northeast dealers sell some version of these five plans (will-call, auto, pre-buy, cap, budget). Connecticut specifics: CT

The five programmes, decoded

Will-call
You call when you need oil and pay the dealer's price that day. Some set a minimum order: Cubby's is 100 gallons.
Day rate
Auto-delivery
The dealer schedules fills from the weather and your usage. Pricing differs by dealer: Smart Touch takes $0.03/gal off for a 12-month automatic commitment. Ask yours whether automatic is priced the same as will-call.
Varies
Pre-buy fixed
Lock a price for a contracted gallon volume. A pre-buy is paid in full before the season; Santa Energy says paying up front gets a lower rate, and Dead River calls its Prepaid Fixed plan its lowest price per gallon.
Locked
Cap
A not-to-exceed price: if the daily price falls below it, you pay the lower price. What it costs differs by dealer. The Mid-Hudson buyers' group 2026-27 Bottini cap charged $0.35/gal up front; Petro and Burke charge no sign-up fee but $399 to leave the one-year plan early; Santa Energy and Cubby charge a fee they do not publish.
Cap + fee
Budget
Equal monthly payments, settled once a year. It spreads the cost; it does not change it. Petro's SmartPay is no-fee; Santa Energy's Even Pay runs 11 payments.
Monthly

Connecticut pre-buy consumer protection

Connecticut General Statutes §16a-23 to 23p require surety bond for residential pre-paid heating oil contracts. Strongest state-level protection in NE. Regulator: CT DEEP + Department of Consumer Protection (DCP).

Connecticut dealer landscape

31% of CT homes heat with oil, the highest oil-heat penetration in the lower NE. CT DEEP publishes a separate weekly survey.

Which programme suits a 275-gal first-time buyer?

A first-winter household typically wants will-call (no commitment) plus auto-delivery sign-up halfway through to avoid emergency fills. Pre-buy is for households with a year of usage data. Cap is for households that want a price ceiling and have asked the dealer what the cap costs.
