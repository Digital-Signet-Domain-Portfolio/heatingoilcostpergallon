Massachusetts all-time high

Massachusetts's post-2020 peak was the 2022-23 season at $5.71/gal, driven by the Russia-Ukraine distillate shortage. The 25-year nominal high was 2008, around $4.50/gal in Massachusetts residential.

vs PADD1A
