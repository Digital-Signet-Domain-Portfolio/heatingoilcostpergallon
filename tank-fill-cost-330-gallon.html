Outdoor tank cold-flow

Outdoor 330-gal tanks need attention below 5°F. Straight #2 begins gelling around 10°F. Either a kerosene blend (typically 80/20 #2 to K-1) or a cold-flow additive prevents gelling. See heating oil vs kerosene.

Minimum delivery rules

Most NE dealers will not deliver less than 100 gallons. Outdoor 330 tanks at 20% (66 gallons remaining) are borderline. Schedule a top-off rather than risk a no-deliver.
